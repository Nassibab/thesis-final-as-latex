\section*{Declaration on the Use of Generative AI}

Generative artificial intelligence tools were used as supporting tools
during the preparation of this Master's thesis.

Their use included, where applicable, support with literature discovery,
brainstorming and structuring, language and style improvement, critical
review of written material, and assistance with software development
and coding tasks conducted as part of the research.

Generative AI outputs were not treated as independent academic sources.
Academic claims and evidence were verified against the original sources
where applicable.

AI-generated suggestions, analyses, text, and code were critically
reviewed, selected, modified, or rejected by the author.

The author remains fully responsible for the research design,
methodological decisions, interpretation of results, argumentation,
conclusions, and final content of this thesis.

The use of generative AI tools was conducted in accordance with the
applicable requirements of Munich University of Applied Sciences and
the principles of good scientific practice.
